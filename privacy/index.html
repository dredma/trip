Nimbo

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 18 November 2026

The short version

  • Nimbo works fully without an account. Your flights, notes, ratings, photos and settings stay on your phone.
  • There are no ads and no advertising trackers. Anonymous crash reports are sent only if you answer Yes when the app asks, and anonymous usage statistics only if you opt in (both can be changed in Settings → Privacy).
  • Aircraft photos are off by default; if you turn them on, only the aircraft registration or type is sent (Settings → Aircraft pictures). Live flight lookups and community averages happen only when you ask for them.
  • Everything else online (account, friends, cloud backup, trip links, anonymous community statistics) is off until you turn it on. Switch it off and delete the server copy at any time in Settings → Friends & cloud.
  • The app is not directed at children. You must be 16 or older to create an account.

What is stored on your phone

Flights, trips, notes, memories and photo copies, seat and flight ratings, booking references, cached flight data and your settings. Your sign-in session is kept in Android's encrypted storage. Nothing of this is sent to us. Android device backup (Google's automatic backup of app data) is switched off for all of this. The only exception is one small file with the Pro trial start date and a "purchased" flag, which Android may include in your own Google backup so that reinstalling does not restart the free trial; it contains no flights or personal details. Use Settings → Export / backup to keep a copy of your data. "Wipe this phone" in Settings → Privacy deletes it.

Nimbo Pro (one-time purchase)

The basic app is free forever. Optional Pro extras come with a 7-day free trial and can then be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's own privacy policy; the app never sees your card or payment details, and Google does not give us your name, email or payment data. The app reads from Google Play on your phone whether you own Pro; that purchase status is not sent to our server, except the optional Pro badge flag described below. To remember the trial the app stores only its start date and a "purchased" flag on your phone (not sent anywhere). On start the app asks Google Play which purchases this Google account owns ("restore"). No purchase analytics are added. Ending the trial never deletes or locks your data: your flights, backups and all exports (CSV, JSON, GDPR export) stay free. Live tracking of upcoming flights (background status checks, gate/delay alerts) is a Pro feature; without Pro the app makes no background live lookups, so no flight numbers are sent to the live-data service for that. If you use the optional friends feature and have bought Pro, your profile stores a "Pro" flag so friends can see a PRO badge next to your name. Only accepted friends can see it; switch off "Show Pro badge to friends" in Settings and the flag is set to false. Trial users are never shown as Pro. For app review and testing we can unlock Pro for a specific account on our server ("Pro grant": the account, a short reason such as "store review" and an optional end date). It is deleted with the account and included in "Export all my data". Your colour theme choice is stored only on your phone.

Optional online features

Account, friends and sharing (Supabase). If you create an account we store your email address and a hashed password, your handle, display name and emoji avatar, your friend connections and privacy choices, the statistics you publish to friends (totals, routes, stickers and your flight log without notes, seat, booking reference, ratings or photos), emoji reactions you give to or get from friends on recent flights (visible only to the two of you; deleted when you unfriend or delete your account), and flights you send to a friend (route, date, airline, flight number, aircraft, times – never notes, seat, booking reference, ratings or photos). A friend sees none of this until you choose to: each friend in your friends list has a sharing switch, off by default, which you can turn on or off at any time; Friends → Privacy lets you choose each part per friend. Legal basis: your consent at sign-up and providing the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and (b)). Meet-up alerts (off by default) are computed on your phone from data friends already share; nothing extra is sent.

Trip links (Supabase, only when you tap Share trip while signed in). We store, per flight of the trip: flight number, airline, departure and arrival airport, scheduled times and date, plus an optional name you choose to show (e.g. "Kristian"). Anyone who has the link can see this and the live status (times, delays, gate, terminal) on a web page at nimbo.fly/trip until the link expires 12 hours after your last flight lands (we use the latest known arrival time; if unknown, the scheduled one). You can turn a link off at any time in Settings; it stops working immediately. Expired and turned off links are deleted automatically. Notes, seat, booking reference, ratings and photos are never included. To show live status our server asks AeroDataBox for the flight number and date only – no data about you or the viewer. The web page uses no cookies, trackers, analytics or third-party fonts; GitHub (which hosts the page) and Supabase see the viewer's IP address in their server logs, and to prevent abuse we keep a shortened, keyed hash of it (the key changes every day) for at most one hour.

Cloud backup (Supabase, only when you turn it on or tap "Back up now"). A copy of your flights including notes, memory text, seat, booking reference and ratings, readable only by your account. Photos are not uploaded.

Anonymous statistics (Supabase, only after you agree in a dialog). Per flight: origin and destination airport, month, distance, aircraft type and airline – not linked to your account, filed under a random code kept on your phone. Switching it off deletes what you contributed. If a phone stops contributing, its contributions are deleted automatically 26 months after its last upload. When you agree, the server stores the date and the version of the consent text under your random code, as a record of your consent. Only flights marked Verified (matched against flight data) or Evidence (boarding pass or booking import) are sent, together with that level, which the server checks and does not store. The app also leaves out flights that fail plausibility checks (overlapping, impossible duration, not flown yet, same airport twice, more than 8 a day); those checks run on your phone and nothing about them is uploaded. Switch it on or off in Settings → Friends & cloud → Contribute anonymised flights.

Community averages (Stats screen). If you contribute, the app loads the anonymous community averages and top lists from our server when you open Stats; if you do not, only when you tap Show community stats. The request contains the year only (if you contribute, also your own totals for that year, used for your percentile and not stored). Supabase sees your IP address in its server logs.

Flight verification. To mark a flight Verified, its flight number and date are looked up once through our flight-data service (the same lookup used for live status; the provider sees the flight number and date, not who you are). The result is stored only on your phone.

Supabase Inc. hosts this data as our processor under its Data Processing Addendum. Region of the server: AWS eu-west-2 (London, United Kingdom); the UK is covered by an EU adequacy decision. Supabase may use sub-processors outside the EU under Standard Contractual Clauses.

Live flight data (via the app's server). When you look up live times or gates, the app sends the flight number and date (or an airport and a short time window) to our Supabase Edge Function. That server holds the AeroDataBox API key and asks AeroDataBox on your behalf. AeroDataBox may be located in the USA and acts under its own privacy policy; it receives only the flight number and date (or airport and time window) from our server, never your IP address. Your IP address appears in our provider's server logs, and for rate limiting we keep a shortened, keyed hash of it (the key changes every day) for at most one hour.

Aircraft photos (off by default; only after you tap Show photos on a flight or switch on Settings → Aircraft pictures → Load aircraft photos online; switch off at any time). Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). The aircraft type or registration is sent to Planespotters.net, Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons to find a photo. These services see your IP address; no account data or personal details are sent. The app identifies itself to them with the developer's contact address, not yours.

Sharper satellite detail when zoomed in (off by default; Settings → Map imagery). The satellite map is bundled with the app and works offline without sending anything. Only if you switch this on, zooming in close downloads extra map tiles of the area you are looking at from NASA GIBS (Global Imagery Browse Services, gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov), run on US government servers in the USA. This is a transfer of your IP address to a third party outside the EU/EEA, which you consent to by switching the setting on (GDPR Art. 49(1)(a)); US government servers are not covered by an EU adequacy decision for this purpose. NASA then sees your IP address and which map tiles (area and zoom level) are requested, plus a generic app User-Agent. Nothing else is sent: no account, flights or location. Tiles are cached on your phone (at most 200 MB) and can be deleted with "Clear map cache"; switching the setting off stops all requests. Imagery (bundled and downloaded): NASA Earth Observatory – Blue Marble Next Generation (Reto Stöckli), July 2004, public domain.

Crash reports (only if you say Yes)

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends a technical report to Sentry (Functional Software Inc.), which stores it in its EU region (Frankfurt, Germany) as our processor under its Data Processing Addendum. A report contains the error message and code location (stack trace), the app version, the phone model, Android version, screen size, language setting and a short list of preceding app events (for example "screen opened" or "network request failed with status 500").

After the privacy notice on first start, the app asks Send crash reports? with equally prominent Yes and No buttons. Reports are sent only if you answer Yes, and never while the switch is off. Installs from before this change are asked once; until they answer, nothing is sent.

Before anything is sent, the app removes flight numbers, airport codes, dates, email addresses, web addresses with their paths and parameters, share-link codes, sign-in tokens and file names. Reports contain no account, no user or device identifier, no screenshots and no IP address (the app does not send it and Sentry is set not to store it). Network request bodies are never included.

Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and, because the report reads details from your phone such as its model and Android version, Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive / Art. 43(4) of the Croatian Electronic Communications Act). The app keeps the date and wording version of your answer on your phone as a record. You can withdraw consent at any time: switch off Settings → Privacy → Send crash reports and nothing more is sent from that moment. Sentry keeps reports for up to 90 days. Sentry (Functional Software Inc.) is a US company; any access from the USA is covered by its certification under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in its Data Processing Addendum as fallback.

Usage statistics (only if you opt in)

Off by default. If you switch on Share anonymous usage statistics (on the first-start screen, in a one-time card, or in Settings → Privacy), the app counts on your phone, per day, which screens and main features are used (for example "Stats opened", "Poster exported") and roughly how long screens stay open, in four ranges only (under 10 seconds, under a minute, under 5 minutes, longer), never the exact time. Once a day it sends the counts of past days to our own server (Supabase), with the app version, the platform and the language and country from your phone's language setting (not your location).

There is no account, user or device identifier, no flights, airports, flight numbers, text you type or exact times. The server only accepts a fixed list of event names and adds the counts to daily totals for all users, so a single person's use cannot be picked out. To prevent abuse it uses a hash of your IP address with a key that changes every day; the IP address itself is not stored and the hash is deleted within a day.

Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and, for reading the counts on your device, Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive). Switching it off stops counting and deletes the counts not yet sent; totals already added cannot be linked to you and so cannot be removed individually.

Boarding-pass scanner (Google ML Kit)

Reading text from boarding-pass photos and tickets happens on your phone with Google's ML Kit. Your images and the recognised text are not sent to Google or to us. ML Kit may send Google technical diagnostics (for example performance, errors and an installation identifier used for these diagnostics) under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Email

Our addresses contact@nimbo.fly and privacy@nimbo.fly are hosted on our own mail server in Croatia. When you write to us we keep your message, email address and our reply to answer you and to document requests about your data (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c) and (f)). Our emails are delivered through Mailjet (France, EU), our processor.

Sign-in codes, password-reset and account-deletion links are sent by Supabase Auth (our processor, see above) to the email address of your account, only when you ask for them.

Inactivity reminder. If you have not signed in for 2 years, we may send one email (through Mailjet) to your account's address saying that the account still exists and how to delete it if you no longer need it. It is sent once per period of inactivity, contains no tracking, and we only record that and when it was sent (deleted with the account). Legal basis: our legitimate interest in not keeping data you no longer want (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

How long we keep data

  • Account data: until you delete your account. Nimbo is a log and backup of your own flights, which people keep for years and may open only rarely, so we do not delete accounts for inactivity. After 2 years without a sign-in we may send you one reminder email (see Email) with a way to delete the account if you no longer want it.
  • Shared flights: 90 days.
  • Trip links: until 12 hours after the trip's last arrival, or until you turn them off (then deleted within a day); live-status cache: 3 days.
  • Cloud backup: until you delete it or your account; markers of deleted flights are removed after 180 days.
  • Anonymous statistics: until you withdraw them, or 26 months after your phone last contributed, whichever comes first.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days at Sentry.
  • Emails to us: 2 years after the last message of the conversation.
  • Requests about your data (access, deletion, objection and so on) and our replies: 5 years after the request is closed, so we can show that we handled them (the longest limitation period for damages claims under Croatian law).
  • Pro grants: until their end date or until the account is deleted.
  • Usage statistics: daily totals for all users for up to 26 months; IP-hashes for rate limiting deleted within a day.

Your rights

You can access and export (Settings → Privacy → Export all my data), correct, and delete your data: Settings → Privacy → Delete my cloud backup or Wipe this phone; Settings → Friends & cloud → Delete my account and data (or, without the app, https://nimbo.fly/delete/). If you delete your account on the website without the app, we cannot find your anonymous community contributions: they are filed only under a random code on your phone, and we hold nothing that links them to you, so we cannot identify them as yours (GDPR Art. 11). They are deleted automatically 26 months after their last upload; to remove them at once, switch off Contribute anonymised flights in the app before you uninstall it. You can withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing before withdrawal), object, restrict processing, and complain to a data protection authority in your EU country (in Croatia: AZOP, https://azop.hr). For anything else, write to privacy@nimbo.fly.
