Privacy: trip links

What this page shows. A traveller using the Nimbo app chose to share a trip with you. The link shows only, per flight: flight number, airline, airports, scheduled times and date, an optional name the traveller chose, and public live flight status (times, delays, gate, terminal). No notes, seats, booking references or other personal details.

How long. The link stops working and the data is deleted 12 hours after the last flight lands (latest known arrival time, otherwise the scheduled one). The traveller can turn it off at any time.

You, the viewer. This page sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and loads no trackers, analytics, ads or third-party fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub and our backend provider Supabase (server in London, UK) see your IP address in their server logs. To prevent abuse we keep a shortened, keyed hash of your IP address (the key changes every day) for at most one hour. Live flight status comes from AeroDataBox; our server sends them only the flight number and date, nothing about you.

Who is responsible. See our privacy policy · privacy@nimbo.fly. You have the rights under the GDPR to access, erasure, objection and to complain to a data protection authority (in Croatia: AZOP).
