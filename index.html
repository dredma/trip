Log flights

Scan a boarding pass, import a booking or CSV, or type it in. The pass is read on your phone. The photo is never stored.

Live status

Times, gates and delays, with no API key to set up. Pro adds gate and delay alerts, plus home-screen widgets.

Maps

Every route on a spinning 3D globe or a flat map, with a route heat map.

Stats & passport

Distance, time in the air, airports, airlines and records. Pro adds country stamps and a year in review.

Friends & community

An optional account lets you share flights with friends. Pro adds friend insights and opt-in community comparison.

Achievements

Pro unlocks badges, avatars and passport stamps for the flying you have already logged.

Share a live trip

Send family a link to follow your trip live. A Pro feature. The link stops working 12 hours after you land.

Export & posters

Export a route poster of your flying. Pro adds calendar export of upcoming flights and a photo backup ZIP.

Private by design

Local-first, with no ads or trackers. Works fully without an account. GDPR, made in the EU. Export or delete your data at any time.

Free, with Pro for life

Nimbo is free to use. Pro is a one-time lifetime unlock, with a 7-day trial — not a subscription.
