FAQ

Is Nimbo free?

Yes. The basic app is free and works fully without an account. Pro is a one-time lifetime unlock, with a 7-day trial — not a subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. Nimbo works fully without an account. Create one only if you want friends, cloud backup or trip links.

Where is my data stored?

Your flights, notes, photos and settings stay on your phone. If you turn on friends, cloud backup or trip links, that copy is hosted by Supabase on a server in London (AWS eu-west-2, United Kingdom). See the privacy policy for details.

Can I import my flights from other apps?

Yes. You can import flights from FlightMemory, App in the Air, Flighty, Logmytrip, OpenFlights, myFlightradar24, or any CSV.

Where do flight times and live status come from?

Live times, gates and delays, and flight verification, come from AeroDataBox, looked up through our server when you ask for them.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Friends & cloud and tap Delete my account and data. You can also delete your account without the app on the delete account page.
