The app

Nimbo is a local-first personal flight log. Your flights, notes, ratings, photos and settings are stored on your phone. The basic app works fully without an account.

Some features are optional and stay off until you turn them on:

  • An account, friends and sharing, hosted by our processor Supabase
  • Anonymous community statistics (off by default; only if you agree)
  • Cloud backup of your flights (photos are not uploaded)
  • Shareable live trip links
  • Live flight times and gates, looked up through our server
  • Crash reports to Sentry and anonymous usage statistics, only if you say yes
  • If you switch them on, aircraft photos and sharper map tiles from third parties

Nimbo is not directed at children. You must be 16 or older to create an account.

Live flight data and accuracy

When you look up live times or gates, or verify a flight, the app sends the flight number and date (or an airport and a short time window) to our server. The server asks a third-party flight-data provider (AeroDataBox) on your behalf. You do not enter your own API key.

Live and historical flight information is provided for personal logging only. It may be late, incomplete or wrong. Do not use it for operational, dispatch, navigation or safety decisions, or for anything where a missed or late flight would cause harm.

The provider’s data is subject to a shared monthly quota and to rate limits. The app enforces a fair-use cap so the quota can be shared among users. We may slow, refuse or temporarily suspend lookups if the quota is exhausted or if use is excessive or automated. We do not guarantee that live data will be available at any particular time.
