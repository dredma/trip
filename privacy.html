Privacy: trip links

What this page shows. A traveller using the Flight Tracker app chose to share a trip with you. The link shows only, per flight: flight number, airline, airports, scheduled times and date, an optional name the traveller chose, and public live flight status (times, delays, gate, terminal). No notes, seats, booking references or other personal details.

How long. The link stops working and the data is deleted 12 hours after the last flight lands (latest known arrival time, otherwise the scheduled one). The traveller can turn it off at any time.

You, the viewer. This page sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and loads no trackers, analytics, ads or third-party fonts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub and our backend provider Supabase see your IP address in their server logs. To prevent abuse we keep a shortened hash of your IP address for at most one hour. Live flight status comes from AeroDataBox; we send them only the flight number and date, nothing about you.

Controller. Kristian Popovic, the developer of Flight Tracker (contact details to be completed). You have the rights under the GDPR to access, erasure, objection and to complain to a data protection authority.
