Delete your account

Your flights live on your phone. Only the optional online account is stored on our server, and you can delete it at any time.

In the app

  1. Open Settings → Friends & cloud.
  2. In Your data on the server, tap Delete my account and data.
  3. Confirm with Delete. Tick Also delete all data on this phone to wipe the phone too.

It happens immediately and cannot be undone.

No app any more?

Enter the email of your account. We send a one-time sign-in link to it, so only you can confirm.

Delete this account?

This deletes your Nimbo account and everything listed below. It cannot be undone.

What is deleted

  • Your account, email and password
  • Profile: name, handle, avatar, published stats
  • Friends, friend requests and privacy settings
  • Shared flights, messages and reactions
  • Shared trip links
  • Cloud backup of your flights

What may be kept

  • Anonymous statistics you chose to contribute are not linked to your account. Deleting in the app removes them too; after a web deletion they cannot be traced back to you.
  • Security logs at our hosting provider (IP address, time) for a few days. A hashed rate-limit counter for up to one hour.
  • Pro purchases: Google Play keeps its own purchase records.
  • Data on your phone stays until you wipe it or uninstall the app.

We keep no other copies or backups.
